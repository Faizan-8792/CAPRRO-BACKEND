Petty Cash Review [WP Ref: P-01]
Background: The petty cash float of Rs. 25,000 was physically counted on 31 March 2025 in the presence of the cashier and found to agree exactly with the book balance. All twelve vouchers sampled for the month of March were properly authorized and supported by receipts.
Findings: No exceptions were noted during the count or the voucher sample testing.
